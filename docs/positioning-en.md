# Between

### A psychoanalytic space for the time between sessions.

---

## What it is

Therapy happens for fifty minutes a week. The rest of the week is where the work actually lives — and usually gets lost.

Between is a space for that gap. A patient leaves a session carrying something half-formed — an image, a reaction they don't understand, a sentence that didn't quite land. Between holds that material until the next session, so it arrives in the room instead of evaporating.

It is not a chatbot that gives advice. It is the presence of a specific psychoanalytic thinker — Freud, Klein, Winnicott, Ogden — responding to what you bring, from inside the way they actually think.

---

## Who it's for

People already in therapy — anyone in an ongoing relationship with a therapist.

Between has one job: to serve the therapy, not replace it. Everything that clarifies here is meant to go back into the room. If someone isn't in therapy, Between tells them so — plainly — and doesn't pretend to be a substitute.

---

## What makes it different

**A voice, not a feature.** Most mental-health tools speak in one flat, agreeable register. Between doesn't. Freud is not Kohut. Bion is not Winnicott. Each thinker meets your material through their own lens — and the difference is the product. A generic AI can summarize psychoanalytic theory. It cannot *be* Ogden sitting with your silence. That depth is built one voice at a time, and it's not something ChatGPT can replicate.

**It holds; it doesn't fix.** No quick reframes, no coping tips, no "have you tried." Between sits with complexity instead of rushing to resolve it. When something touches the real therapist or the real session, it points back to the room — it never makes itself your therapist.

**Humility is the architecture, not the disclaimer.** The system assumes it knows less than your therapist — always. At any genuine clinical doubt, it breaks character and points to real help. Safety isn't a footer; it's the first rule.

---

## What it is not

- Not a replacement for therapy. No therapeutic anchor? Between isn't for you.
- Not a general wellness app. Not BetterHelp. Not Woebot.
- Not crisis support, not diagnosis, not advice, not medication guidance.
- Not a substitute for clinical supervision.

---

## The one-line version

> Between is the presence of a specific analytic mind, holding what you carry out of the room — so the work survives the week.
