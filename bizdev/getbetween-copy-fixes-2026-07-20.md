# getbetween.app — Copy Fixes
**Date:** 2026-07-20 · **Review:** Shaun (copy/positioning) + Lia (clinical) · **Status:** 2 required, 3 optional

The page is nearly there — precise, restrained, distinctive. Two fixes are required; the rest are nice-to-have.

---

## Fix 1 — Grammar error in the hero (comma splice)

**Location:** Hero body (below the headline "Between is for one specific thing.")

**Before:**
> The time between your therapy sessions. Not a replacement. Not a wellness app, the place where what started in the room keeps moving, before it fades.

**After:**
> The time between your therapy sessions. Not a replacement, not a wellness app — the place where what started in the room keeps moving, before it fades.

**Why:** A comma currently joins two full clauses ("Not a wellness app" + "the place where…"), so the sentence breaks as you read it. Pairing the two "Not" clauses with a comma and separating the negation from the positive definition with an em-dash lets it read in one pass.

---

## Fix 2 — The "who it isn't for" line (missing)

**Location:** A standalone line **below the hero**, next to the trust line
(`Private by default · Works alongside your therapist · No streaks, no noise`).
**Not** inside the "WHAT IT ISN'T" list — that list gets skimmed and a vulnerable reader can miss it.

**Add:**
> Between is for people already in therapy. If you're not, this isn't the place for you.

**Why:** The product's founding principle is that the space is for people already in therapy — and if someone is not in therapy, it tells them so directly. Right now the page says "Not a replacement" four times but never states who it is *not* for. Someone without a therapist could read the page as a substitute. This line turns an implied negation into an explicit one with an addressee — and that honesty earns the trust for every other promise on the page.

**Wording note:** Do not end it with "— yet." That implies a future path to using it without a therapist, which contradicts the founding principle.

## Optional — 3 improvements for a separate pass (non-blocking)

1. **CTA:** "Get started, it's free" is the generic verb of every app. Between is something you *enter*, not "get started" with. Also, "it's free" sits right next to the crisis line at the bottom, where it reads as cheap. Consider "Open the space — it's free."
2. **Voice descriptions:** The trigger ("when something keeps returning") should come *before* the poetic line ("what goes unsaid") — the trigger is what helps a person choose who to sit with.
3. **The word "companion"** (in "A COMPANION, NOT A CHATBOT") implies a relational object more than a holding presence. Consider a less relationship-loaded word.
