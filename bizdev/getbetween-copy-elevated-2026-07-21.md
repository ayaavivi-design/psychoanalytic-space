## THE VOICES — make this the heart of the page (move it up)

The single thing no general chatbot offers is sitting with a *specific* thinker. Lead with it.

**Section headline:**
> You don't sit with an app. You sit with a voice.

**Subhead:**
> Freud listens for one thing. Klein for another. You choose whose way of listening the moment needs — not who has the answer.

**Sample dialogue (as live proof, right below):**
> [You] Something my therapist said stayed with me. I don't know if it's true.
> [Winnicott] Where did you feel it, before you decided whether it was true?
> [You] In my chest, I think. Before I could argue with it.

**Closing line under the dialogue:**
> Not advice. Not a diagnosis. A question that keeps you close to your own experience.

**Required clinical fix:** the current line reads "the voice of **someone** who spent a life listening." The word "someone" frames the tool as a person/partner. Replace with:
> Drawn from the thinkers who shaped how therapy listens.
