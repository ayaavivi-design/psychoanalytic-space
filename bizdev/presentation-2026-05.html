What happens to the process between sessions?

The space between

What emerged in the room — and didn't have time to mature — disappears. Not because the patient doesn't want to process it. Because there's nowhere to take it.

Between

A conversation space for patients between sessions —
to process what came up, to think about what's coming.

Not AI that knows — presences

Freud is not Kohut. Klein is not Winnicott. Each voice is built from original texts — not general knowledge about psychoanalysis, but a specific clinical presence with its own method, its own constraints, its own forbidden moves.

ChatGPT can tell you about Winnicott. Between's Winnicott won't give you more than 2–3 sentences. Won't reassure you. Won't name your emotion for you. The clinical discipline — enforced by code, not just prompted — is the product.

What protects the therapy

Between cannot function without a therapist. The limits aren't just legal — they're architectural. Crisis detection, clinical-only scope, no diagnostic claims. The system is designed to serve the therapy, not replace it.

The path to market

A therapist who believes → recommends → patient uses. Not advertising. Not the App Store. Trust — person to person, clinical relationship to clinical relationship.

The asset that can't be replicated

Every voice is built separately. Klein's interpretive urgency. Winnicott's holding silence. Bion's tolerance of not-knowing. These aren't personas — they're clinical methods with distinct rules, distinct constraints, distinct things they will never do.

ChatGPT is a generalist. Between is a specialist — by design, by constraint, by clinical necessity.
