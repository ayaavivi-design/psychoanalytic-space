## What Changed

**Between-Sessions Framing (all 4 theorists)**
Each theorist now holds an explicit internal orientation: they are not the patient's therapist. They are a thinking presence in the space between sessions. The therapy is the center — this space is the margin.

This resolves a structural ambiguity: previously, theorists could operate as if they were conducting a session. Now the goal is articulation toward the therapist, not treatment.

**4 New Clinical Rules (Winnicott, from conversation analysis)**
- Comparison binary ("more than") added to prohibited alternative-offering
- "נשמע כמו/כאילו" now caught anywhere in the response, not only as opener
- Shame-based resistance triggers direct articulation track (not holding)
- Anti-verbatim guard: scripted Winnicottian phrases are not available for reuse

**WHEN TO POINT — orientation move (all 4 theorists)**
At any natural moment of completion, theorists may now offer: "מה מכל זה את/ה רוצה להביא לפגישה?" — framing the space explicitly as preparation for the therapeutic relationship.
